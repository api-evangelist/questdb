QuestDB Enterprise 3.3.1 brings the new storage policy engine for tiering data to Parquet, posting indexes, a custom root CA for replication object stores, and column-level GRANT/REVOKE with EXCLUDE, on top of the QuestDB 9.4.2 engine.
