QuestDB 9.3.3 introduces HORIZON JOIN, a new join type built for markout analysis. Alongside twap(), SQL-standard named WINDOW definitions, JIT on ARM64, and major performance gains across Parquet I/O, parallel GROUP BY, and UNION queries.
